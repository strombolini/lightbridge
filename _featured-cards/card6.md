<p>In Ethiopia’s highland regions, rugged terrain often separates villages from clinics and the internet alike. LightBridge Foundation is installing offline AI kiosks in health‑posts and church compounds to bridge that gap.</p>

<p>The kiosks converse in Amharic and Oromo, providing guidance on prenatal care, vaccination schedules, nutrition, and safe water practices. Local health extension workers use the AI as a teaching aid, while families can ask private questions in a judgment‑free space—strengthening community health, one village at a time.</p>

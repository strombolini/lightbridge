Around the world, **seven in ten people living in low-income countries remain offline**, and millions lack access to even the most basic health and education resources.  
Yet **four in five of those same communities have reliable electricity**—enough to power a small, affordable computer.

LightBridge Foundation was created to close that last-mile information gap.  
We build and ship **offline “AI libraries”**—palm-sized devices that run powerful, multilingual language-models locally. People can simply speak or type a question and receive accurate, up-to-date guidance on health, farming, education, and entrepreneurship *without any internet connection*.

---

## Our Mission

> **To empower underserved communities with trustworthy, locally relevant knowledge—delivered through low-cost, offline artificial intelligence.**

---

## Why It Matters

- **400 million** people globally still lack access to basic health services such as maternal care, child immunizations, and safe water.  
- **70 %** of households in the world’s lowest-income nations remain unconnected to the internet, limiting opportunities to learn, earn, and stay healthy.  
- **$100 USD** is all it takes to install a complete LightBridge kiosk in a village center—far less than traditional computer labs or building new schools.

---

## What We Do

| Pillar | How We Act |
|--------|------------|
| **Deploy AI Libraries** | We load compact LLMs (e.g., DeepSeek, Llama variants) onto fanless mini-PCs or ARM boards with a screen and microphone. Each unit serves an entire village. |
| **Localize & Translate** | Content is available in regional languages—Chichewa in Malawi, Bangla in Bangladesh, Yoruba and Hausa in Nigeria, Amharic in Ethiopia, and more. |
| **Partner & Train** | We work with schools, clinics, farmers’ co-ops, and faith centers to install kiosks and train local stewards to keep them running. |
| **Iterate Ethically** | Models are vetted for bias, updated regularly, and tuned to provide safe medical and agricultural guidance with clear disclaimers. |

---

## Strategic Focus Areas

1. **Health & Well-being**  
   Portable kiosks deliver maternal-health checklists, vaccination schedules, nutrition advice, and disease-prevention tips—saving families days of travel to distant clinics.

2. **Education & Youth Skills**  
   Students prepare for exams, learn coding basics, and discover entrepreneurship—all in their native languages and at their own pace.

3. **Climate-Smart Agriculture**  
   Farmers receive season-specific advice on crop selection, flood-resilient practices, and fair market prices, boosting food security and income.
